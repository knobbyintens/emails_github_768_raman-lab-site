Priya Raman, Daniel Okafor, Hana Sato

*ICML 2026 (accepted).* SparseFlow schedules token routing across experts using structured block sparsity, cutting expert activation cost while keeping load balance within 2%. The paper reports 2.7x inference speedups for 8x7B mixture-of-experts models on A100 GPUs.
